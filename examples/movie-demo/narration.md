# vidkit movie mode — narration

The score is mixed under the narration spans `verify.json` reports, and those
spans come from these lines. Nothing here is a picture: the words are what the
card shots set in type, and the `solid:` and `still:` shots carry no voice of
their own.

## Scene 1 — A film from a specification · 0:00–0:04

**Every picture in this film is declared. Nothing was captured, nothing was fetched, and no browser ran.**

## Scene 2 — A declared field · 0:04–0:07

**This shot is one flat colour, and the camera moves across it because the specification said to.**

## Scene 3 — A declared picture · 0:07–0:09

**A still is a claim that something was made. The file has to exist, or the build stops.**

## Scene 4 — An expressed clock · 0:09–0:12

**These lengths are written down rather than measured, so the report says the clock was expressed.**

## Scene 5 — Closing · 0:12–0:15
